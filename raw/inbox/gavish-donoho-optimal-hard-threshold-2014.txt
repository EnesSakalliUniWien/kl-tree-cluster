Title: The Optimal Hard Threshold for Singular Values is 4/sqrt(3)
Authors: Matan Gavish and David L. Donoho
Version checked: arXiv v3, last revised 2014-06-04

Relevance note:
The paper derives asymptotic MSE-optimal hard thresholds for singular values
in low-rank matrix denoising. For square matrices with known noise level, the
threshold is (4/sqrt(3)) sqrt(n) sigma; with unknown noise, a median singular
value rule is used. This is relevant to KL-TE Q14/Q15 as a candidate
rank-selection threshold that is more conservative than the ordinary MP upper
edge, but the objective is denoising risk rather than selected-tree null
false-split control. Treat it as diagnostic input, not as production
justification for an MP/projection-dimension rule.
